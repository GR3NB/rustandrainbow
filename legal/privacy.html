Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 9, 2026  |  Business: Rust & Rainbow  |  Contact: rustandrainbow@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Rust & Rainbow ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with our Etsy shop, our social media presence on Instagram and TikTok, and our use of platform APIs including the RustandRainbowClaude TikTok application (TikTok App ID: 7638050043181959175), the Meta Instagram Graph API, the Etsy Open API, the Printify API, and the Ideogram API.

1. Who we are

Rust & Rainbow is a small print-on-demand business selling apparel and accessories. We do not operate a customer-facing web application. We do not collect personal data from end users beyond what Etsy, Instagram, TikTok, and our payment processors handle on our behalf under their own privacy policies.

The RustandRainbowClaude application is a content scheduling and publishing tool used by Rust & Rainbow to post marketing content to TikTok.

2. Information we receive

We do not directly collect personal data from visitors to this page. The only personal data we receive comes from:

  • Etsy: order details (buyer name, shipping address, items ordered) supplied by Etsy when you purchase from our shop. Etsy's privacy policy governs how Etsy collects and shares that data.
  • Printify: the same order details, forwarded by Etsy or by us, so that Printify can print and ship your order. Printify's privacy policy governs Printify's handling of that data.
  • Instagram and TikTok: publicly visible engagement data (likes, comments, follower counts, video view counts) that we may read using the Meta Instagram Graph API and the RustandRainbowClaude TikTok application to evaluate the performance of our own posts. We do not access private messages, private profile data, or data about other people who interact with our content beyond what is publicly visible on those platforms.

3. How we use information

We use order data only to fulfill your order. We use platform engagement data only to decide what to post next. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data with anyone.

4. API tokens and credentials

The RustandRainbowClaude application obtains access tokens from TikTok in order to publish content from our automation scripts. Those tokens authorize action only on accounts owned by Rust & Rainbow. They do not grant any access to other users' accounts or data.

5. Cookies and tracking

This page does not set cookies, run analytics, or track visitors. If you visit our Etsy shop, Etsy's cookie and tracking policies apply.

6. Children

Our products are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Third-party services

The following third-party services may receive data in connection with our operations: Etsy (orders, payment), Printify (fulfillment), Meta (post publishing and analytics on accounts we own), TikTok via the RustandRainbowClaude application (post publishing and analytics on accounts we own), Ideogram (image generation, no personal data sent). Each operates under its own privacy policy.

8. Your rights

If you placed an order with us via Etsy and want a copy of, or deletion of, the order data we hold (which is limited to order fulfillment information), contact rustandrainbow@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Data retention

We retain order fulfillment data for as long as required by tax and accounting law. Engagement and analytics data from Instagram and TikTok is retained only as long as it remains useful for content decisions, typically less than 90 days.

10. Security

We use platform-provided OAuth flows and store credentials in environment files that are not shared or published. We do not maintain a customer database.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top reflects the most recent change. Material changes will be noted on this page.

12. Contact

For privacy questions: rustandrainbow@gmail.com
