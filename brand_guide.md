# Rust & Rainbow — Brand Guide

## The Brand

**Name:** Rust & Rainbow  
**Handle (all platforms):** @rustandrainbow  
**Etsy Shop:** RustAndRainbow  
**Tagline:** *Velcro dogs. Proud owners. Zero apologies.*

---

## What This Brand Is

Rust & Rainbow sells print-on-demand products (t-shirts, mugs, stickers, tote bags) for gay vizsla owners and the broader gay dog parent community. The brand lives at the intersection of breed-specific humor, gay identity, and Pacific Northwest energy.

It is not a generic pride brand. It is not a generic dog brand. The specificity is the point.

---

## Core Audience

**Primary:** Gay men who own vizslas or other high-energy breeds. Age 30-50. Treat their dog as a family member. Active on Instagram and TikTok. Willing to spend $25-40 on something that feels made specifically for them.

**Secondary:** Vizsla owners broadly (regardless of orientation). The velcro dog humor and breed-specific designs cross over.

**Tertiary:** Gay dog owners of any breed who connect with the gay dog dad identity.

---

## Brand Voice

**Tone:** Dry, warm, unguarded. Sounds like the funniest person at brunch who also genuinely loves their dog more than most humans.

**Never:** Corporate, generic, preachy, overly earnest about pride in a way that feels performative.

**Examples of on-brand copy:**
- "Built for the field. Currently velcroed to my leg."
- "Two dads. One vizsla. Zero personal space."
- "Gay agenda: coffee, dog walk, brunch, repeat."
- "My dog has separation anxiety. So do I. We're working on it."
- "Part hunting dog. Part barnacle. Full chaos."

**Examples of off-brand copy:**
- "Celebrate love in all its forms!" (too generic)
- "Show your pride with this amazing tee!" (too salesy)
- "Because every dog deserves a loving home." (too earnest)

---

## Visual Identity

**Color Palette:**
- Rust / burnt sienna (#B5451B) — vizsla coat color, warm and distinctive
- Warm cream / off-white (#F5F0E8) — background, clean but not cold
- Rainbow accent — used sparingly, not slapped on everything
- Forest green (#2D5016) — PNW nod, occasional use
- Deep charcoal (#2C2C2C) — for type, clean and modern

**Design Aesthetic:**
- Minimalist line art and flat illustration
- Retro/vintage poster style (70s earth tones work well)
- Watercolor for softer pieces
- Bold typography for humor/text designs
- NO: clipart, busy backgrounds, drop shadows, gradient abuse

**What every design should feel like:** Something a gay dog dad would buy at a Portland farmer's market from a local illustrator. Warm, specific, a little witty.

## Niche Design Pillars

Every design fits into one or more of these:

1. **Velcro Dog** — vizsla clinginess humor
2. **Gay Dog Dad** — two dads, proud owner, gay identity + breed
3. **Hungarian Chaos** — breed-specific energy/personality
4. **PNW Dog Life** — Oregon/Pacific Northwest dog culture
5. **Pride + Breed** — rainbow integrated into breed illustration, tastefully
